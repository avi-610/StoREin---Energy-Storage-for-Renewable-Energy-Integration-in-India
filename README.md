# StoREin-Energy-Storage-for-Renewable-Energy-Integration-in-India
Indo-German development cooperation project supporting viable energy storage systems at distribution grid level in India,
implemented by GIZ in partnership with MNRE, Government of India.

**Overview**
The StoREin project is an Indo-German development cooperation initiative implemented by GIZ GmbH on behalf of BMWK (Germany) under the International Climate Initiative (IKI), 
in close cooperation with MNRE, Government of India.

**Business Problem**
India's rapid expansion of renewable energy has created significant challenges around grid stability, energy storage, and distribution-level integration. 
Despite growing renewable capacity, the absence of viable energy storage frameworks, regulatory clarity and technical expertise at the distribution grid level limits effective renewable energy utilization. 
This project was designed to address these gaps through policy development, technical research, and on-ground demonstration.
